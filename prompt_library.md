BLOG — RGCCO + Style Transfer
Optimized Blog Prompt

Write a blog post about why remote work is beneficial using RGCCO (Role, Goal, Context, Constraints, Output) and Style Transfer.

Role: Act as an experienced workplace productivity writer.
Goal: Explain the benefits of remote work to professionals and organizations.
Context: Cover productivity, flexibility, employee satisfaction, cost savings, and work-life balance.
Constraints: Use a professional, informative, and engaging style similar to a modern business productivity blog. Keep the article between 600–800 words, use clear headings and practical examples, maintain a balanced tone, and briefly acknowledge potential challenges.
Output: Provide a complete blog post with a title, introduction, headings, and conclusion.
Style Transfer: Follow the tone and structure of a modern business productivity blog.
