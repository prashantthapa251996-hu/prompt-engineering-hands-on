1. Blog
Objective

Create an informative and engaging blog post explaining the benefits of remote work for employees and organizations.

Issue with the Before Prompt

The Before prompt — “Write a blog post about why remote work is good” — is too general. It does not specify the target audience, writing style, topics to cover, desired length, structure, or whether challenges should also be discussed. As a result, the output is relevant but fairly general.

After Strategy

The optimized prompt uses RGCCO (Role, Goal, Context, Constraints, Output) combined with Style Transfer. It defines the writer's role, target audience, required topics, word count, structure, professional style, and balanced perspective.

The Before version scored 16/20, while the After version scored 20/20. The improvement came from the optimized prompt producing a structured article with a clear title, headings, practical examples, balanced discussion, and conclusion.

3. Email
Objective

Issue with the Before Prompt

The Before prompt — “Write a sales email promoting our new water bottle” — does not provide enough information about the product, audience, tone, structure, features, or desired length. This results in a basic sales email without strong product-specific details.
